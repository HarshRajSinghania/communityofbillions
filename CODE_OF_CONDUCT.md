# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in communityofbillions to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this community:

- Assuming good faith and asking before concluding
- Giving and accepting specific, actionable feedback
- Saying "I don't know" and "I was wrong" out loud
- Focusing on what is best for the protocol, not for winning an argument

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Pressure to add a token, a sale, or a financial product to this project

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when an individual
is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report abuse to the maintainer via the contact method on the GitHub profile
[@withinaz](https://github.com/withinaz). Reports are reviewed and handled confidentially.

Consequences escalate with severity: a private warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban.
Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may face the same consequences.
